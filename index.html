🐠 Charlie's Fish Finder

Difficulty is a list, not a wall. Pick a fish, see its actual problems, check them against your tank — then decide which problems you're happy to own.

The method (read once, then use the app)
1Name the problems "Intermediate" is a rumour. A list of specific problems is information. Every fish here has its difficulty broken into named problems.
2Sort each problem SETUP solve it once, before you buy. HABIT a routine you keep doing. RISK something to watch for and have a plan B for.
3Check against YOUR tank A problem for someone else's tank may not be one for yours — and the reverse. Your shrimp and snail change the answer.
4Decide, don't default Mark each problem: I can own this, I'd need to learn, or dealbreaker. Dealbreakers are allowed — but they're chosen, not assumed.
